# ASHHEART — The Mines of Dunmere

A pixel-art action roguelite for the browser. Dive 100 floors below the town of Dunmere, keep its lamps burning, and silence the Heart of Ash.

## Play
**Play online: https://rblackwood224.github.io/AshHeart2/**

Open `index.html` in a modern browser, or host the folder on GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → deploy from the main branch).
Online co-op needs the page to be served over https (GitHub Pages works).

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard (P1) | Keyboard (P2) | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD | Arrows | Left stick / D-pad |
| Attack / use / mine | J | Num1 | A |
| Heavy attack | K | Num4 | X |
| Dodge roll | Space (or Shift, L) | Num2 | B |
| Skills | 1, 2, 3 | Num5, Num7, Num8 | Y, LB, RB |
| Potions | Q (life), E (mana) | Num0, Num. | D-pad up / down |
| Inventory | Tab | Num+ | Select |
| Menu / back | Esc | NumEnter | Start |
| In menus | Arrows + Enter, Esc to go back | | D-pad + A, B |

**Options** are grouped on tabs: Game, Video, Audio, Controls and Account (switch tabs with Q / E, LB / RB, or a click).

**Auto-target (keyboard and controller):** attacks and skills turn to the foe in the direction you face or steer, else the nearest one, and stay on the foe you are fighting. A rock or cracked wall right in front is mined, not skipped. Moving the mouse hands aiming back to the mouse; Options → Auto-target turns it off.

**Your own controls:** Options → Controls → Keys and buttons. Every keyboard and every controller keeps its own layout; Reset to default brings the one above back.

**Mouse (PC, Diablo style):** left click the ground to walk (hold to steer), left click a monster to walk up and attack it (hold to keep attacking), left click a chest, door, stairs or person to use it, right click for a heavy attack toward the cursor, left click an ore or crystal rock to mine it, drag an item out of the backpack (or onto the bin) to drop it, drag it onto another slot to move it, Shift + left click attacks in place. Skill keys aim at the cursor. Menus can be clicked too.

Phones and tablets get touch controls automatically (play in landscape).

## Languages
English, Français and Magyar — switch under Options → Language (the first start follows the browser language).

## Highlights
- 100 floors in 10 biomes, a boss every 10 floors, ASHHEART on floor 100, then endless depths and a daily time attack.
- 30 item sets (2/3/4/5-piece bonuses and unique set powers), legendary and mythic items, runes and runewords.
- Four story quest chains from the people of Dunmere, with unique rewards.
- Mining: coal, copper, iron, silver, gold and mithril rocks (deeper floors hold better ore) plus crystal rocks. Any weapon breaks them. The smith needs the right ore for each tier; coal feeds your torch.
- 15 achievements under the RECORDS tab. Ore upgrades and reforges gear at the smith, crystals add sockets.
- Gravestones: where a hero falls, a grave stays on that floor. Read it to see who died there, to what, and with which stats.
- Six classes: Paladin, Rogue, Wizard, Necromancer, Barbarian and the co-op-only Priest. The Necromancer raises fallen monsters as servants, curses foes and makes corpses explode; the slain stay on the floor for a while. Servants have no time limit: they stand until destroyed, mend slowly out of combat and follow you down the stairs; Raise Dead ranks, servant-life gear and passives make them tougher. The Barbarian has rage instead of mana — hitting and getting hit fill it, it fades out of combat — and leaps, roars and slams the ground. He has his own gear (maul, horned helm, fur armor) and two sets. The **Priest** plays only in co-op (local or online): he heals the most wounded friend, shields, sanctifies the ground, raises the fallen from afar and burns the undead; healing fills his Faith, and full Faith pays for a Prayer that strengthens the whole party. He has his own island in the passive tree and two sets. A Necromancer can dismiss his servants from the pause menu.
- A passive tree of about 310 points with three long branches and a keystone per class, plus shared outer paths. Levels come slower the deeper you go.
- Another class's part of the tree costs 2 points per node and its keystones are theirs alone. Skill books only drop for your own class (plus Whirlwind, Mend and Spirit Wolf for everyone); skills an older hero learned from another class's books work at half power for double cost.
- More than 90 kinds of creatures, among them beetles, serpents, moths and ogres in every world.
- Locked chests hold better loot: buy or find a lockpick (the Rogue needs none). You carry at most 6 healing and 4 mana potions; spells cost more mana as you level.
- Nemesis system, legendary beasts, floor events, bounties, a town that remembers what you did.

## Endgame
- **Paragon:** after level 70, experience turns into paragon levels. Each gives a point for main stat, vitality, damage, crit, attack speed, armor, damage taken, gold/magic find or move speed (pause menu → Paragon; taking a point back is free).
- **Ancient items:** legendaries and set pieces sometimes drop as ancient (orange-red): every number about 30% higher and at least one socket. Harder difficulties drop more of them.
- **Gems:** ruby, sapphire, emerald, topaz, amethyst and diamond in four grades (chipped, plain, flawless, perfect). They do different things in a weapon, a helm or armor. Use a gem in the bag to set it into gear with a free socket.
- **Isolde the jeweler:** three gems make a better one; she also takes gems out of gear in one piece.
- **Kazimir the gambler:** pick a kind of item, pay, see what you get.
- **Doran the smith** now also salvages gear into iron scrap, arcane dust and legendary shards, rerolls one bonus of an item, and adds a new bonus. Clearing sockets gives your gems back.

## A living Dunmere
- The town **grows** (Book of Dunmere: Y on the bounty board, later the guild hall): slain bosses, bounties, helping its people, broken raids and gold donations build new homes, the jeweler's shop, the gambling den, stone houses and the guild hall.
- **Eight people with names:** talk to them, give one gift a day (each likes something else), take their tasks (bring things, slay creatures, find a lost item on a given floor). Friendship hearts bring gifts, and good friends make the shops cheaper.
- When hope (trust) stays low, people **leave**; when it rises, they come back. In the evening they gather by the inn, at night they go home.
- **Town events:** raiders waiting on the next floor you enter, a traveling trader with rare wares, or a festival with lower prices and a blessing.
- Retired season heroes stand as **statues** by the well.

## Weekly challenge (Weekly Depth)
Title → Weekly Depth. The same mine and three rules for everyone this week (one hard, one kind, one strange — about 30 rules in all). A fresh level 1 hero, one life, 45 minutes on a countdown clock: get as deep as you can. Play as often as you like; your best counts. Floor 10 or deeper sends a chest to your town stash. Up to 3 players count as a team (separate table): on one machine, or online — Online co-op → Host weekly depth / Join weekly depth (everyone picks a fresh class; the code is the same 5-letter room code). Every player of the team sees the result and can submit it. Options → Game → Weekly challenge hides it for those who just want to play in peace.

## Seasons
Every 8 weeks a new season with its own rule (Ashen Winter, Blood Moon, Season of Bones, Gold Rush, Time of Shadows, Call of the Deep, Storm Season, Return of the Ancients). When creating a hero choose **Normal** or **Seasonal**. Seasonal heroes start fresh, play only with seasonal heroes, and in their mine the Ashheart **pulses**: every 10 minutes a minute of raging monsters and flowing treasure. Together all seasonal heroes dig the **shared shaft** (milestones: more gold, more experience, the Heart Chamber, more magic find). At floor 30 a hero earns the season's cloak colours; the season's Heart drops the season's legendary item. When the season ends: stay a normal hero, or become a **legend** — a statue in town, an heirloom in the stash of every other hero of yours, and +3% experience for all of them.

## Leaderboard
Weekly results (solo and team) and season heroes go to a leaderboard that lives in this repository. At the end of a run press **Submit to the leaderboard**: GitHub opens with everything filled in, press *Submit new issue* (a free GitHub account is needed). A small GitHub robot checks the code, adds it to `board/data.json` and closes the issue, usually within a minute. The robot creates the `board` folder by itself, so it is not part of the game files: when you upload a new version, never upload or delete the `board` folder, or the leaderboard starts again from zero. A player name belongs to the GitHub account that first used it; rude names are refused. The board is shown in the game (title → Weekly Depth → Leaderboard, Options → Account, or X on the bounty board).

**Setting it up (once, for the owner of the repository):** upload the `.github` folder (with `workflows/leaderboard.yml` and `scripts/board.js`) and the `board` folder; then Settings → Actions → General → Workflow permissions → *Read and write permissions* → Save. Issues must be switched on (Settings → General → Features).

## Graphics
Options → Smooth motion (computers, on by default): the camera and moving things step a third of a pixel, so walking and scrolling stay smooth on 60 Hz screens while the pixel art stays sharp. A slow machine steps it down by itself.

Options → Spectacular lights: normal-mapped pixel lighting, walls and monsters cast shadows, glow and vignette. On by default on computers, off on phones (it costs more power). Toggle it any time.

## Difficulty
Pick it at the mine entrance in town (only there): Normal, Hard, Extreme, then Brutal I–IV. Every step brings more monsters (up to twice as many on Brutal IV) with more life and damage, and you more experience, gold, magic find and elite packs. In co-op the host's difficulty counts; the daily time attack is always Normal.

## Permadeath
One life only: if your hero falls for good, the save is gone. Once per floor a killing blow leaves you at 1 life (Last Breath) — a second mistake on the same floor is the end.

## Co-op
- **Local:** up to 3 players. Title → Local co-op: every controller (or keyboard) presses A / Enter to pick a hero, then A again to start. You can also join mid-game: press START on another controller (or NumEnter for the second keyboard player) to join with your own hero. Together you share one screen; wander apart and it splits, with arrows pointing at your friends.
- **Online:** Title → Online co-op → Host a game opens a lobby with the 5-letter room code. Friends choose Join a game and type the code; they wait in the lobby until the host starts. Nobody can join a game that has already started. The host runs the game; everyone keeps their own loot and their own save.

## Music
Drop your own tracks into the `music` folder: `title.mp3`, `town.mp3`, `mine.mp3`, `mine2.mp3`, `mine3.mp3`, `boss.mp3`. The mine plays its tracks one after another. Missing files are simply skipped. Music and sound volume are under Options.

## Saves
Heroes are saved in the browser. To move a hero to another device: **Save code** on the hero (a text code to copy, and a QR code to scan with a phone), then **Load hero from code** on the other device. **Export file / Import hero file** still work too. Loading a newer code of the same hero updates it.
